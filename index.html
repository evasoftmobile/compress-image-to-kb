Privacy Policy

Compress Image to KB

Last updated: September 13, 2026

EVASOFT Mobile respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Compress Image to KB application.

1. Image Processing

Images selected in Compress Image to KB are processed locally on your device. EVASOFT Mobile does not upload your selected images to its own servers.

EVASOFT Mobile does not collect, store, view, or share the images that you select for compression through its own servers.

2. Advertising

Compress Image to KB uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and related purposes. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google services configuration, this information may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information.

You can learn more about how Google handles information by visiting Google Privacy Policy .

3. Consent and European Users

Compress Image to KB uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent where required.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other applicable regions may be presented with privacy and consent choices regarding advertising and data processing.

Where required and available, users can review or change their privacy choices through the application's privacy options.

4. Permissions and Photo Access

Compress Image to KB only accesses images that you choose through the Android image selection system or other system-provided selection mechanisms.

Selected images are used for the compression operation requested by you. The application does not require broad access to your entire photo library when Android system photo selection mechanisms are available.

5. Saving and Sharing Images

Compressed images are processed locally on your device.

When you choose to save an image, the resulting file is stored on your device using Android storage mechanisms.

When you choose to share an image, Android's system sharing functionality is used to send the selected compressed image to the application or service chosen by you.

6. Data Storage

EVASOFT Mobile does not operate an image-processing server that stores the photos you select or compress with Compress Image to KB.

Third-party services used by the application may process information according to their own privacy policies and your applicable privacy and consent choices.

7. Third-Party Services

Compress Image to KB currently uses the following third-party Google services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and applicable consent choices.

8. Children's Privacy

Compress Image to KB is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically directed at children.

EVASOFT Mobile does not knowingly collect children's personal information through its own servers through this application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

Changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised when appropriate.
